About

I have spent more than ten years selling technology and environmental solutions to enterprises, financial institutions and governments in Brazil and Latin America. Since 2023 I have been building Saltica, a climate risk intelligence company for banks, insurers, funds and municipalities, where I own P&L, sales, partnerships and product.

Before that, I managed strategic accounts of R$ 10M+ ARR at Superbac — Cargill, Coamo, C.Vale, Banco do Brasil — and long-term carbon and nature-based projects with World Bank and IDB stakeholders at Ecosecurities. I am a biologist (B.Sc. and M.Sc., UFMG) and a 2026 Halcyon LAC Climate Intensive Fellow.
